01 / About me

A little
background.

From Sichuan University
to ELTE.

I’m a software engineering graduate continuing my studies in autonomous systems, with experience in both academic projects and industry.

I completed my bachelor’s at Sichuan University in Chengdu, in an English-taught program supported by a Belt and Road Initiative scholarship. I’m now pursuing my master’s at Eötvös Loránd University in Budapest.

My academic work has included log anomaly detection, computer vision, and comparative deep learning experiments. At ELTE, I’ve contributed to a medical imaging data pipeline, working with DICOM records, annotations, and validation reports.

I enjoy working with others, taking initiative when needed, and helping a team stay organized. I value clear communication, constructive feedback, and following through on commitments.
