LIDC-IDRI Dataset Pipeline

A Python pipeline for DICOM inventory, CT series detection, XML annotation parsing, and validation. The work focuses on turning a complex medical imaging collection into a consistent, auditable dataset for further analysis.

My contribution: contributed to DICOM inventory, CT series detection, XML annotation parsing, identifier matching, and validation. Generated structured manifests and reports, including unmatched annotation references, to support later cleaning and model comparisons.

Team indexing run: 1,010 patients and 244,527 DICOM records; the full run selected 1,018 valid CT series with no processing failures.
